# Phase 1 Experimental Report

## Overview
Phase 1 focused on validating the baseline T-FAN configuration and exploring the impact of blade pitch adjustments. Two experiments were executed using the 0.8 m rotor assembly.

## Key Findings
- Increasing blade pitch from 12.5° to 14° raised torque output by approximately 7.6%, but reduced stall margin by 2.3 percentage points.
- Efficiency remained above 89% for both configurations, satisfying the preliminary acceptance criteria.
- Vibration levels stayed within the design envelope, confirming mechanical integrity of the prototype rig.

## Recommendations
1. Transition to the 0.92 m rotor for Phase 2 to recover stall margin while maintaining torque gains.
2. Expand instrumentation with strain gauges to capture localized stress under higher pitch loads.
3. Correlate empirical stall margin with the theoretical bound derived in Section D of the updated supplement.
